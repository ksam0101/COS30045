Appliance energy in Australia

Why household appliance energy matters

Every appliance in the home adds to the electricity bill. Understanding which choices make a difference helps households spend less and use energy more wisely.

🏠

Many appliances, one bill

Fridges, heating, cooling, laundry and entertainment all draw power. Some run all day, others only when used, so both power and habits matter.

⭐

The Energy Rating label

Many appliances sold in Australia carry a star label and an estimated energy figure, so shoppers can compare similar products before buying.
