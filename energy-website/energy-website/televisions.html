Bigger screen, bigger bill?

A guide for Australian households choosing a new TV: what the official energy data says about size, star ratings and screen technology, and how to work out your own running cost.

Who this story is for

Households buying or replacing a TV

The audience is everyday shoppers rather than engineers. They are comparing models in a shop or online, they care about the electricity bill, and they want to know what actually changes the numbers so they can decide quickly. Each section below answers one of their questions in plain language, with one chart per message.
